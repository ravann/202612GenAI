# RB-03 Mail relay queue backlog

Applies to the outbound mail relays `mail-relay-01` and `mail-relay-02`. Both resolve names through `dns-01`.

## Symptoms

- Outgoing mail arrives late or not at all.
- The relay queue holds more than 1,000 messages.
- Deferred messages show status `451 4.7.1` (try again later) or `421` (service not available).

## Check the queue

1. Run `relayctl queue status` on each relay. Note the number of active and deferred messages.
2. Group the deferred messages by destination domain with `relayctl queue top-domains`.
3. If one domain holds most of the queue, the receiving side is throttling us. If all domains are deferred, the problem is on our side (DNS, disk or network).

## Receiving side throttles us (451 4.7.1)

1. Lower the sending rate to that domain to 10 messages per minute with `relayctl rate set <domain> 10`.
2. Wait 30 minutes and check the queue again. It should shrink steadily.
3. Restore the normal rate once the queue is below 100 messages.

## Our side is failing

1. Check that `dns-01` answers MX lookups from the relay.
2. Check free disk space on the relay. Below 10% free, the relay stops accepting new mail.
3. If one relay is unhealthy, take it out of rotation; the other relay can carry the full load for a working day.

## Bounces with 550 5.7.26

This means the receiving domain could not authenticate us. Check that the SPF record on `dns-01` lists both relays.
