# RB-12 Legal hold on mailboxes

**Classification: restricted.** Only change managers and the legal operations team may read this runbook. (Course fixture: all details are fictional.)

## What a legal hold is

A legal hold stops any deletion of a person's mail, including mail that the person deletes themselves, until legal operations lifts the hold.

## Placing a hold

1. Legal operations raises a request with a matter number such as `LH-2026-014`. Do not act without a matter number.
2. Enable the hold on the person's mailbox in the mail archive. Do not tell the person.
3. Confirm that the archive copies of mail sent through `mail-relay-01` and `mail-relay-02` are kept.
4. Record the matter number, the mailbox and the time in the request. Do not write the reason for the hold.

## While a hold is active

- Do not delete or move the mailbox, even if the person leaves.
- Retention clean-up jobs skip held mailboxes automatically. Check this after any change to the archive.

## Lifting a hold

Only legal operations can lift a hold, in writing, with the same matter number.
