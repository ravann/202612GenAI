# RB-05 Change management policy

This policy applies to every change to a production asset in the CMDB.

## What every change request must contain

1. A title that names the action and the asset, for example "Fail over db-04 to db-05".
2. A description that explains why the change is needed.
3. The affected assets, by CMDB ID.
4. The linked incident, when the change fixes or works around an incident.
5. A risk rating: `low`, `medium` or `high`.
6. A rollback plan that says how to undo the change and how long that takes.
7. A planned start time in UTC, inside a change window.

A request that misses any of these items is sent back to the requester.

## Risk ratings

- `low`: one asset, no customer impact expected, rollback under 15 minutes.
- `medium`: customer-facing service or shared asset, rollback under 1 hour.
- `high`: several services affected, data migration, or rollback over 1 hour.

## Approval

- Every new change starts in the status `pending_approval`.
- Only a change manager may approve a change.
- Nobody approves a change they requested themselves.
- An AI assistant may draft and propose a change request. It never approves one.

## Change windows

- Standard window: Tuesday and Thursday, 20:00 to 23:00 UTC.
- Emergency changes may run outside the window if a P1 incident is linked and a change manager approves the change by phone first. The change request is still recorded.

## After the change

The requester records the result in the change request within one working day and closes the linked incident if the change fixed it.
